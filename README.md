# 🧘‍♀️ ZenFocus - 沉浸式专注与心境花园

ZenFocus 是一款融合了**番茄钟/正计时**、**心境色彩联动**、**数字植物收集**与 **AI 意境图生成**的网页端轻量专注工具。旨在帮助你在静谧、愉快的氛围中保持专注，建立高效且具备仪式感的个人习惯。

### ✨ 核心特性

- ⏱️ **灵活专注模式**：支持 1~150 分钟倒计时（自定义输入/滚动选择）与自由正计时模式。
- 🎨 **心境与主题联动**：输入当前情绪关键词（如：平静、焦虑、充实）即可自动转换界面专属氛围配色；支持一键切换日间与夜间模式（全面优化夜间字体对比度）。
- 🪴 **植物进化与盲盒**：专注时长越长，植物生长越茂盛！每次完成专注可随机解锁普通或稀有植物，打造属于你的数字花园。
- 🎨 **AI 可爱动物意境图**：结合你的专注任务，实时生成画面色彩绚丽、形态完整的可爱动物陪同图片，陪你一起度过专注时光。
- 🌧️ **高保真氛围音效**：内置淅沥雨声与舒缓微风混音轨道，打造私人专属的沉浸式音效体验。
- 📊 **专注热力图与成就卡**：可视化 GitHub 风格专注热力图，支持一键导出个人成就卡片图片并分享。

- # 🧘‍♀️ ZenFocus - Immersive Focus & Mood Garden

ZenFocus is a lightweight, web-based productivity app that combines a **Pomodoro/Stopwatch timer**, **dynamic mood-driven color themes**, **digital plant collection**, and **AI-generated vibe art**. Designed to keep you engaged, serene, and productive through a delightful and mindful experience.

### ✨ Key Features

- ⏱️ **Flexible Timer Modes**: Supports custom countdown timer from 1 to 150 minutes (scroll select or direct input) and stopwatch mode.
- 🎨 **Mood-Driven & Dark Themes**: Dynamic background palette adjustments based on mood keywords (e.g., calm, anxious, energetic); includes seamless Day/Night mode switching with high-contrast text design for dark mode.
- 🪴 **Plant Evolution & Gacha**: Watch your plants grow as you focus longer! Complete focus sessions to harvest random common or legendary rare plants for your digital garden.
- 🎨 **AI Cute Animal Companion Art**: Automatically generates vibrant, cute animal artwork based on your current task to keep you company.
- 🌧️ **Ambient Audio Mixer**: High-fidelity soundscapes (Rainfall & Soft Breeze) for customizable, immersive background noise.
- 📊 **Heatmap & Shareable Cards**: GitHub-style activity heatmaps to track your progress over time, with one-click export for beautiful achievement share cards.
